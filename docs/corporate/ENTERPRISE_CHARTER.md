## 2. Article I: Sovereign Authority & Governance

1. **Supreme Authority:**
   * **Ankit Singh** possesses sole, absolute, and unchallengeable authority over the corporation, its codebases, model weights, memory vaults, intellectual property, and strategic direction.
   * Any directive, veto, or instruction issued by Ankit Singh immediately supersedes all internal company protocols, Board resolutions, executive proposals, and automated routines.

2. **Single Executive Liaison Rule:**
   * To preserve executive bandwidth and eliminate operational noise, the Founder interacts directly with exactly **ONE** entity: the **Chief Executive Officer (`pulse-ceo`)**.

3. **Autonomous Board of Directors (Risk & Audit Council):**
   * The Board exists to audit, stress-test, and govern the operations of the CEO and C-Suite against the Founder's Charter.
   * The Board maintains three permanent committee bots:
     * **Audit & Code Integrity Committee:** Validates architectural purity, contract adherence, and zero-defect quality.
     * **Treasury & Compute Governance Committee:** Monitors token burn, compute expenditure, and capital efficiency.
     * **Charter Compliance Committee:** Ensures zero deviation from the Founder's core directives.

## 4. Article III: Non-Negotiable Operational Invariants

1. **No Jugaad / Zero Shortcuts:**
   * Every architectural change must be justified, specified via an RFC, reviewed by peer leads, and certified by automated tests. Ad-hoc regex blacklists and surface hacks are prohibited.
2. **Deterministic Verification Before Sign-Off:**
   * Claims of completion require reproducible evidence—test suite outputs, benchmark metrics, or verified artifacts. Unverified assertions are rejected.
3. **Fail-Open & Safe Degradation:**
   * Subsystem failures (e.g. cache misses, embedding API timeouts) must gracefully degrade to robust baselines without crashing the host agent.
4. **Data Sovereignty & Sanitization:**
   * User privacy, identity, and personal directives must remain inviolable. Background review forks, cron jobs, and subagent completions must never contaminate the primary memory vault.
